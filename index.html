Who is the Púca?

Who is the Púca?

In Irish folklore the púca is a shapeshifter. It walks the roads at night as a black horse, a goat, a hare, a hound. It can bring ruin or good fortune, and you never know which until it is too late. On Samhain the farmers leave the last of the harvest for it, just in case.

We took its name because we change shape too: one studio, three of us, a new form for every game.

Our story

Born under Benbulben

Púca Games comes from Sligo, in the north-west of Ireland. It's a county of old stories: Queen Maeve's cairn on Knocknarea, the passage tombs of Carrowmore, and the flat-topped mountain on the horizon of this page, Benbulben, which Yeats asked to be buried under.

We make games the way the old storytellers worked: short, intense and built to be remembered. We would rather give you five unforgettable minutes than fifty forgettable hours.
