Who is the Púca?

In Irish folklore the púca is a shapeshifter. It walks the roads at night as a black horse, a goat, a hare, a hound. It can bring ruin or good fortune, and you never know which until it is too late. On Samhain, the farmers leave the last of the harvest for it, just in case.

Our story

Born among the standing stones

Púca Games comes from Sligo, in the north-west of Ireland. It's a county of old stories: Queen Maeve's cairn on Knocknarea, the passage tombs of Carrowmore, and the hills and lakes Yeats kept writing about. We grew up on those stories and on the games that kept us up too late.

We're a small, young team. We make games the way the old storytellers worked: short, intense and built to be remembered. We would rather give you five unforgettable minutes than fifty forgettable hours.

Right now that means games for the Arkade Gaming Gun, where you point a real gun at the screen and the game has to answer with precision. Next, the old world. The púca is patient.
